You walk towards the mountains you guys were at yesterday, you hear the voice calling your name louder and louder. When suddenly the path you once knew like the back of your hand is now becoming unfamilliar, the sun is setting faster than normal. You hear one last yell, and you run towards the direction when you find yourself in front of a giant mountain.

You've never seen this before, it opens up and reveals a staircase where at the end you see a pile of bright shiny gold. You hear your moms voice again, she begging you to come up and join in her the discovery you guys have made. But something is wrong, you feel wrong. It feels wrong. As you stand at the bottom the voice that once sounded like your precious mother now is deeper, scarier, demanding. "COME HERE, NOW. HURRY" starts to repeat and echo all around the mountains. You feel a gust of wind pushing you closer and closer, you put one hand on the rail when suddenly the hairs in the back of your neck stand up. The figure at the top that was waving at you is now racing in all fours towards you. You turn around and book it as fast as your 10 year old legs can possibly take you.
